Naturalness feedback

AudioLLM feedback directly optimizes synthesized-speech naturalness, while pointwise-seeded iterative pairwise ranking further refines the reward used for GRPO.

The idea

Pointwise feedback, locally refined.

01

Group sampling

Generate multiple candidate utterances for the same target text.

02

Pointwise seeding

Use an AudioLLM score to establish a coarse initial ordering.

03

Pairwise refinement

Compare nearby unseen candidates in two sparse Swiss-style rounds.

04

Ordinal GRPO

Convert the refined ranking into bounded group-relative rewards.

Base AudioLLM-Point PSIPR Ours

For each language, we pool Seed-TTS and CV3-Eval, retain samples where bidirectional SpeechJudge consistently prefers PSIPR to AudioLLM-Point, rank by the identity-aligned mean score margin, and randomly draw seven from the top 20 with a fixed seed. Per-sample scores are hidden to avoid biasing listening.
