Beyond MOS Predictors: AudioLLM Feedback for Naturalness-Oriented TTS Reinforcement Learning

Chu Zeng1, Wenrui Zhang1, Pingyi Fan1, Wei-Qiang Zhang1*

1 Department of Electronic Engineering, Tsinghua University, Beijing, China

zengc25@mails.tsinghua.edu.cn · wqzhang@tsinghua.edu.cn

Abstract

Reinforcement learning (RL) for text-to-speech (TTS) commonly relies on automatic-speech-recognition objectives or fixed mean-opinion-score (MOS) predictors, motivating richer, rubric-conditioned feedback for naturalness-oriented optimization. We propose using an audio-language model (AudioLLM) as a naturalness reward for TTS RL. Across bilingual zero-shot benchmarks, AudioLLM feedback improves naturalness as assessed by two held-out AudioLLM judges while maintaining competitive intelligibility relative to a dedicated CER–NLL reward, despite using no explicit recognition objective. We further introduce Pointwise-Seeded Iterative Pairwise Ranking (PSIPR), which refines pointwise AudioLLM scores through sparse bidirectional comparisons of closely matched samples. This pairwise refinement consistently improves pointwise feedback and achieves higher naturalness scores than the DNSMOS P.808 reward baseline under both judges in every language–dataset condition. Our results demonstrate AudioLLMs as effective perceptual feedback for TTS post-training, with pairwise comparison providing an additional improvement.
